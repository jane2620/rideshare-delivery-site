Request your Uber and Uber Eats Data

Click the button below to request your data and follow the instructions in Uber's privacy data. If you get lost, return to this page for more detailed instructions.

Request your data

(Data requests typically take 24-72 hours to process)

Detailed instructions

  1. Navigate to https://myprivacy.uber.com/exploreyourdata/download. You will be required to log in to your Uber account.
  2. Return to https://myprivacy.uber.com/exploreyourdata/download after logging in (if you are not automatically redirected).
  3. Click “Request”. Note that you may have to wait 24–72 hours for your data to be ready. In our experience, this is closer to 24 hours.
  4. You will receive a notification via email when your data is ready. You can search for the email using this query: from:noreply@uber.com "data is ready for download"
