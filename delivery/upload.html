Delivery Data Files

You should have requested your delivery history data from the platforms you use in Phase 1. If you have not, please do so now. Data requests often take 24-72 hours for the platforms to process.

I need to request my data

Follow these links to download your data. You will have to log in to your accounts.

  1. Request Grubhub data. After logging in, click "Submit Request" under "Get a copy of your personal information".
  2. Request DoorDash data After logging in, click "Request Archive" under "Account Data".
  3. Request Instacart data. After logging in, select "Request Type: Access My Personal information" and click Submit.

Finding Emails from Platforms

When your export is ready, the platform will email you. In your email associated with each platform you have an account with, paste one of these into the search bar to find the email:

  • DoorDash
    • from:no-reply@doordash.com "Your DoorDash data archive is ready for download"
  • Grubhub
    • from:help_me@eat.grubhub.com
  • Instacart
    • from:noreply@m.onetrust.com "Your Privacy Request Has Been Updated" "Instacart"

Follow the instructions in the email and in your browser to view and download your data requests. You may have to log into your account.
