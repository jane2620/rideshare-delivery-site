Understanding Rideshare and Delivery Platform Algorithms

Princeton University IRB #18415

Study Overview

Thank you for participating in our research study! We're interested in learning more about rideshare and delivery platforms, and how people use them. In this study, we are interested in Uber, UberEats, Lyft, Doordash, Grubhub,and Instacart.

What You'll Be Asked to Do

We will provide instructions and links to each phase of the survey to your email. As part of this study, you will be asked to complete four phases. You are currently in Phase 2, 3 or 4.

Phase 1: Onboarding (Completed)
  • Learn about the study
  • Complete onboarding survey
  • Time: approximately 10 minutes
Phase 2: Screenshot collection (2 weeks)
  1. Take screenshots of ride/delivery requests
  2. Order ride/delivery
  3. Receive ride/delivery
Phase 3: Request data
  • Request ride/delivery history from platforms you use
  • Time: approximately 10 minutes, and requires a 24-72 hour processing period
Phase 4: Data upload
  • Organize and upload screenshots
  • Upload rideshare/delivery files
  • Time: approximately 10 minutes

Phase 2: Qualifying Screenshots

Qualifying screenshots must capture (1) the estimated time of arrival, (2) the service you selected (e.g., select Priority if you will order Priority), and (3) the pricing details of the order. For more information on what screenshots to upload, see the examples below.

Some platforms may require more than one screenshot to capture all of the required information (see UberEats, DoorDash below). You may also upload screenshots from desktop/web browsers if you prefer.

Compensation

Participants who complete all phases of the study will receive compensation as follows:

  1. $10 for uploading your ride/delivery history and at least 5 screenshots of your ride/delivery orders.
  2. $1 bonus for each additional screenshot you upload (>5 total), up to $10. For example, if you upload 10 screenshots, you will receive $15.

Data Privacy

Your data will be uploaded to a secure server, with access limited to the research team. You'll be able to review all of your data prior to uploading it to us.
